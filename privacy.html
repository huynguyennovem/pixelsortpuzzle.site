Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Pixel Dunes (the “game”), published by HugeTree (“we”, “us”). The short version: the game has no accounts, it stores your progress only on your device, and it uses two Google services that collect data from your device: Google AdMob to show ads, and Google Analytics for Firebase to measure installs, sessions, level completions and ad revenue. Both may use your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers.

1. What we collect ourselves

We don’t ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email address, location or contacts, and the game has no server of ours that receives your gameplay data. We do receive aggregated reports from Google Analytics for Firebase (section 4), which Google builds from the data it collects through the game.

2. What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, current board, and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the puzzle and results screens, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded videos when you ask for a hint. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app name and version);
  • information about how ads are shown and interacted with.

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google’s own privacy policy, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob data disclosure for details.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers ourselves, and the ad network does not send any personal data back to us.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game includes Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. We use it to count installs and sessions, to see how far players get and how often ads appear, and to measure the ad revenue the game earns so we can evaluate and optimise our advertising campaigns on Google Ads. It collects automatically, without any action from you:

  • an app instance ID and your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • usage events such as first open, session start, screen views, and ad events (ad format, ad impressions and the estimated revenue per impression);
  • events sent by the game itself: each completed level (level number, moves, hints and undos used, time taken) and each time the game requests, shows or fails to show an ad (with the level it happened on).

Apart from these, the game does not send your puzzle progress or other gameplay events to Firebase. Google processes this data under its own terms; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. This data can be linked with Google Ads so we can see which campaigns brought in players. We don’t sell it and it is not used to identify you by name.

5. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and similar regions): the first time you open the game you may see a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asking whether ads can be personalized and whether data can be used for measurement. Google Analytics for Firebase follows your choice. You can review or change your answer at any time from the Privacy button in the game’s menu. In regions where no consent form is shown, analytics and ad measurement run by default.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalization, in Settings › Google › Ads (menu names vary by device).
  • No ads, no analytics: turn off your connection — the game works fully offline and neither shows ads nor sends analytics without one. Data collected while offline may be sent once you reconnect.

6. Purchases and other SDKs

The game has no in-app purchases, so no payment information is involved. The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust how often ads appear without an app update; it downloads these settings using a Firebase installation ID and sends no gameplay data. Apart from Google’s advertising SDK, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Remote Config and the consent tools they use, we have not added any crash-reporting, attribution or social-media SDKs.

7. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

8. Data retention and security

We don’t hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies. Your on-device progress is protected by your device’s own security.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the store listing and the game.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the game? Email hugetree29@gmail.com or use our support page.
