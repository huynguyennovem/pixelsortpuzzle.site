Why you'll like it

Simple to learn, hard to put down

A classic sorting puzzle, rebuilt with solid pixel, sand and crystal instead of liquid — and a handcrafted pixel-art look.

One-tap sorting

Tap a source tube, then a destination. The matching layers pour over by themselves — no dragging, no fiddly aiming.

Always solvable

Every level is generated and checked by a built-in solver, so you're never stuck on a board that can't be finished.

Free undo & hints

Undo as many moves as you like, restart any time, and ask for a hint when you're stuck — a hint is always granted.

Chase three stars

Beat a level in as few moves as the solver's PAR to earn all three stars. Stars are just for bragging rights — nothing is locked behind them.

Short sessions, offline

Levels take a couple of minutes, and the game plays fully offline — on the bus, on a flight, anywhere.

Earthy pixel-art

Grainy pixel in glass tubes, gold-framed menus, a chunky pixel font and tiny 8-bit sounds that keep it cozy.

How to play

Three taps to get it

Pick a tube

Tap the tube you want to pour from. It lifts and glows white so you know it's selected.

Pour it over

Tap where it should go. Pixel pours onto an empty tube, or onto the same color if there's room.

Sort them all

A level is done when every tube holds just one color. Empty tubes are your best tool — use them wisely.
